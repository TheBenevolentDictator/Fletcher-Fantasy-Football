    📊 Connect Your Google Sheet

    Sync all 6 tabs directly from your live Google Sheet into the site with automatic updates whenever you edit your spreadsheet!

    1
    Publish your Sheet to the Web:
    In Google Sheets, go to File > Share > Publish to web and click Publish.
    2
    Ensure Share Permissions:
    In Google Drive, ensure general access is set to "Anyone with the link can view".
    3
    Check your Tab Names:
    Make sure your Google Sheet contains these 6 tab names:
    • MASTER
    • Weekly Points
    • WeeklyPtRanks
    • ExpW-L
    • WL Heatmap
    • SoS
    4
    Paste your Google Sheet ID or URL:
    You can paste the entire Google Sheets URL or just the ID string between /d/ and /edit.
